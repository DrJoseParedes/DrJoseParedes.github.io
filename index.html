Introduction

About Me

I am a Lecturer at the University of Roehampton, London, UK. My research focuses on the application of millimetre-wave (mmWave) radar technology for environmental analysis — including tracking individuals and developing navigation systems for drones.

I have a strong background in signal processing, machine learning, and embedded systems, and I am passionate about teaching and mentoring students in engineering and computer science.
